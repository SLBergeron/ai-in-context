# The one prompt

Here's my ICP: [who they are, where, how big, who signs, what's going on when they need me].

Build me a lead list of the best 25 people who match. Use the connected tools. Give me the table in the format from AGENTS.md, then tell me how many matched in total and what you'd change.

## Sample ICP (for the live demo)
Property management companies in Ontario with 11 to 200 employees. The owner, president or director of operations signs. They manage rental buildings for other owners, they're buried in tenant emails and maintenance requests, and they're hiring because of it.
