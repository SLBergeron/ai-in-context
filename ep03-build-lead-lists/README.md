# Ep 03: Build lead lists

From [AI in Context](../README.md) Ep 03, October 22, 2026. Start with 25 people, not 2,500.

## The steps

1. **Write your ICP.** One paragraph: who they are, where, how big, who signs, what's going on when they need you. Example in [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md).
2. **Make a project** in Claude or ChatGPT. Paste [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) in as the project instructions.
3. **Connect Apollo.** Claude: Settings, Connectors. ChatGPT: Settings, Apps. Free: 100 credits a month.
4. **Paste the prompt** from [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md) with your ICP. You get your best 25 people in a table.
5. **Paste the table into a spreadsheet.** One row a person.
6. **Check every name.** Drop anyone retired, gone, or not a fit.
7. **Reach out.** Email or LinkedIn. Short, true, one ask: "Interested?" Follow up twice.
8. **Do it again tomorrow.** 50 a day beats 500 once.

## How many people you need

Work back from the clients you want:

| Want | Need |
|---|---|
| 4 new clients a month | 16 calls (1 in 4 signs) |
| 16 calls | 40 replies (40% book) |
| 40 replies | 2,700 people (1.5% reply) |

That's about 130 people a weekday. Under 2% reply means fix the copy.

## Tools and what they cost

Start at about $30 a month. My setup is about $150.

| Job | Tool | Cost (Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Find people | Apollo | 100 free credits a month, then about $59 for 2,500 |
| Find people, cheaper | Apify Leads Finder | $5 free a month, then about $1.50 per 1,000. Expect half to bounce |
| Run it | Claude, ChatGPT or Codex | Your plan |
| Send at volume | Instantly or Smartlead | Domains $15 a year, inboxes $5 a month |
| LinkedIn at volume | HeyReach | Paid. LinkedIn caps you at about 200 invites a week |

## When you scale email

- One inbox sends about 30 a day.
- About 5 inboxes per domain. Never your main domain.
- 600 a day = 20 inboxes on 4 domains.
- Do this only after your own inbox gets replies.

## A good sequence

- 3 emails, 5 at most.
- One sequence at a time. No A/B tests at the start.
